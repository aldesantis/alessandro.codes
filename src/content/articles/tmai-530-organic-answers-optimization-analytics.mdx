## Summary

SEO and answer-engine optimization should be treated as one strategy: influence AI and search tools to recommend your brand.

Use one scorecard to track profit from non-brand search, answer engines, and AI brand mentions.

Focus on reliable business results, not vendor estimates, and invest in content that builds future influence.

## Highlights

- Panels of a few mil can be somewhat useful at extremely aggregated levels. Ex: Industry. Even by category in an industry, it falls apart. For a single brand in a category in an industry, it is just crap.
  Even a large panel will see 0.01%, or significantly less, of ChatGPT’s **July 2025** publicly stated 2.5 billion prompts. For every MILLION times a question is asked about your category, a vendor claiming “tens of millions of prompts captured” will catch about 100, and only single digits, at best, will be about you. Worse news? **The monthly “search volume” of any one prompt is effectively one.**
