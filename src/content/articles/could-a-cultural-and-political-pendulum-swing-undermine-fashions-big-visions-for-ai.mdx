## Summary

Public resistance to AI is growing, especially around data centres and claims of human craftsmanship. Yet many consumers already use AI to search and research shopping, even if they rarely trust it to buy for them. Fashion companies are building AI into their operations, so they must weigh its benefits against regulation and public trust.

## Highlights

- This week, then, has provided some concrete evidence of something both close and casual observers have known for a while: that AI has not earned the social permission it needs, in the home market of all three of the frontier AI labs, to justify its outsize influence on stock markets or the large demands it now needs to make for land, resources, and investment to warrant [an expected (but shaky-looking) multi-trillion-dollar IPO](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-06/anthropic-ipo-valuation-expectations-pressured-after-ai-rout).
