## Summary

Brian Eno, a prominent figure in modern music, has had a significant impact on the sound and direction of the industry. From his early days with Roxy Music to his work as a producer for artists like David Bowie and U2, Eno has consistently pushed the boundaries of sound. In a diary entry from December 1995, Eno reflects on the relationship between technological imperfections and artistic expression. He argues that the failures and limitations of mediums can actually become cherished and emulated, as they signify something beyond the medium’s capabilities.

## Highlights

- Note to the artist: when the medium fails conspicuously, and especially if it fails in new ways, the listener believes something is happening beyond its limits.
