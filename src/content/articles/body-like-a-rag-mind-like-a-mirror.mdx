## Summary

Ordinary life can feel boring and tiring, so we often create drama to escape it. The teaching “body like a rag, mind like a mirror” invites us to do everyday tasks humbly and notice our thoughts without clinging to them. This can bring calm and love into ordinary life.

## Highlights

- From this point of view, doings do themselves seamlessly, transitions from one task to another are nothing if not poetic. To say this is to go beyond Jocko Willinck’s _good_ because that _good_ is said with resistance in his heart. Here, washing the dishes, sitting with a 2-year-old in the ER, or taking a call is “body like a rag.”
  This rag-body is nearly, beautifully transparent. It’s approaching pure energy.
