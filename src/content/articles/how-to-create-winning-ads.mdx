## Summary

Winning ads focus on urgent, specific moments when a customer feels a problem most. Turn those moments into attention-grabbing hooks and test different versions. The newsletter also shares email savings tips, ways to grow sign-ups, and top commerce news.

## Highlights

- Start with the problem your product solves.
  Then ask: _When does that problem become hardest to ignore?_
  One product will have many acute pains attached to it. Here’s a bunch of examples.
  **Skincare:** Breakout before a first date.
  **Deodorant:** Body odor during a hug.
  **Haircare:** Flakes on your black blazer.
  **Makeup:** Mascara running at wedding.
- Choose one product, then …
  1️⃣ Identify three acute pains
  2️⃣ Write three hooks for each
  3️⃣ Test them in the ad account
  Paper towel ads don’t need to explain why clean floors matter. They just show you the red wine hitting the carpet.
  Find your customer’s spill.
