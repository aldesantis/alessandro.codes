## Summary

Anatta is a fast-growing enterprise Shopify agency that promotes AI-powered delivery and quick projects. Its marketing relies on frequent content, partner events, and one strategist’s strong LinkedIn presence, while senior leaders post little. The report suggests Nebulab could compete by sharing weekly Shopify insights and building stronger referral ties with Shopify’s sales team.

## Highlights

- Positioning: “the enterprise Shopify agency that runs on agentic delivery”, with work “in weeks, not quarters”. Shopify Platinum status is the proof they lean on.[1]
- Their “Retainer Report” targets brands whose agency retainer is “up for renewal or underperforming”.
