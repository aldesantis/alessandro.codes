## Summary

Entrepreneurs use effectual reasoning, which focuses on available resources and lets goals emerge, unlike causal reasoning that follows fixed plans. They prioritize affordable loss, build partnerships, and embrace surprises as opportunities. This approach allows them to adapt and innovate, shaping the future rather than just predicting it.

## Highlights

- Causal reasoning starts with a goal and finds the best way to achieve it. Effectual reasoning starts with available resources and lets goals emerge along the way.
