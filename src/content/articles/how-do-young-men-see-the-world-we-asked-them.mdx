## Summary

Young men in America feel frustrated and powerless, often reacting conservatively to societal changes. Many express resentment towards political narratives that seem to overlook their concerns, especially during discussions about gender issues. This sentiment is further complicated by feelings of victimization and isolation in their personal lives.
