## Summary

AI is changing education and work, so society must rethink its rules. We should value care, cooperation, and shared resources over competition. By learning from people, nature, and future generations, we can shape a fairer future.

## Highlights

- I know that any social organism, including bacteria and us, are conditional cooperators. You cooperate when there is some benefit to cooperate. Otherwise, you compete. So are we cooperators or are we competitors? It depends on the environment we are in. We see it in sports. And schools can be the same.
