## Summary

Technology, especially AI, can take away our real human connections and make us feel isolated. We need to value hard, messy, and slow human experiences that build true relationships and resilience. To fight this loss, we must choose joy in being with others and living fully in the world.

## Highlights

- It doesn’t matter if you hate blackberries and tomatoes, gardening and wading; everyone has their own version of deep immersion in the moment, of engaging with the world in an embodied and sensual way, whether it’s dancing or dog-walking, cake-decorating or dirt-biking. What does matter is that we are beset with the ideology of maximising having while minimising doing. This has long been capitalism’s narrative and is now also technology’s.
