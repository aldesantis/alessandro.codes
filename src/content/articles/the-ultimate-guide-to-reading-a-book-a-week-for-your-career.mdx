## Summary

Cedric Chin shares tips on how to read more books while balancing a busy life, aiming to read 48 books in a year. He emphasizes the importance of reading for career growth and suggests reading three types of books simultaneously: one hard, one easy, and one very easy. To make reading a habit, he advises dropping books you don’t enjoy and choosing the right format for different types of content.
