## Summary

Traditional Chinese businessmen learn best through practical experience and trial and error rather than formal education. Success in business often comes from taking action and learning from mistakes quickly. Trial and error is a valuable strategy in fields where knowledge is limited and circumstances change rapidly.

## Highlights

- My current theory is that business is one such field. The optimal strategy for learning in business is trial and error, because business changes too quickly for there to be immutable rules.
