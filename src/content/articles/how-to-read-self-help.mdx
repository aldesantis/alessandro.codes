## Summary

The document discusses the concept of self-help literature as a form of knowledge that requires practical application and testing, similar to a craft or martial art. It emphasizes the importance of using self-help techniques that are practical and effective, suggesting that not all self-help methods are equally valuable. The text also touches on the idea of backtesting self-help ideas based on personal experiences to see if they would have made a significant difference. Additionally, it highlights the importance of learning from credible sources and testing ideas from believable individuals in the self-help realm.

## Highlights

- I belong to the “your experience is the stupidest way to learn something; it’s much better to learn from other people’s mistakes” camp. You don’t need advice if your life is infinitely long, and if you can run unlimited trial and errors. But we don’t live forever, and most of us don’t have the resources for unlimited trial and errors. So advice matters.
