## Summary

Designers should consider what their work may set in motion, not just what they create. Before building something, ask whether it should exist and what world it could help create. Small decisions can have lasting effects, so we must keep paying attention.

## Highlights

- I run a design studio that works with organizations thinking about their futures. The more I do that work, the more I wonder why we treat the future as something to predict, rather than something our decisions are already making.
- The future rarely arrives with a big announcement. More often, it sneaks in through millions of small decisions that seemed perfectly sensible at the time.
- Perhaps everything we make should carry a warning:
  Caution: may become a world.
