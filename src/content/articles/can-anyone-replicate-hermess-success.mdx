## Summary

Hermès leads the ultra-luxury market with strong sales, exclusivity, and craftsmanship that other brands find hard to copy. Newer luxury brands offer fresh styles and transparency but face challenges matching Hermès’s long heritage and status. The ultra-luxury market grows slowly, with success needing unique products and deep brand trust over many years.

- Regardless of the [narrative that consumers tell about their values](https://www.voguebusiness.com/story/sustainability/why-consumers-dont-shop-their-values-and-how-to-change-that) — that quality trumps all else — status and scarcity ultimately remain at the top, experts say.
