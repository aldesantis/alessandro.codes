## Summary

Building prototypes helps you explore new ideas better than just imagining them. Prototyping reveals hidden opportunities and risks you wouldn’t see otherwise. Understanding grows as you create, not just by thinking or talking.

## Highlights

- It’s best to explore new opportunity spaces through [prototyping](https://gordonbrander.com/notes/prototyping/). To try to model an opportunity space in your head is to explore that opportunity space _in your imagination_. You’ll only find what you already know.
