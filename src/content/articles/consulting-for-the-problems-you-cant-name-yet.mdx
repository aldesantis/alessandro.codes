## Summary

There are two types of consulting: concrete consulting, which is clear and specific, and amorphous consulting, which starts out vague and uncertain. Amorphous consulting helps organizations identify hidden problems through open conversations and observations that insiders may overlook. This type of consulting thrives on uncertainty, allowing the consultant to uncover needs that the organization cannot yet articulate.

## Highlights

- Amorphous consulting starts out fuzzy, illegible, and hard to scope. The parameters of amorphous consulting are so uncertain that it may not appear to be consulting at first. In my experience, amorphous consulting often starts out with undirected conversations (or emails) with someone who may eventually become a client.
