## Summary

Learning through difficulty helps us grow and understand our work. Struggling with other people can build trust and lead to things we could not make alone. Use technology to help us connect, not to avoid learning and human relationships.

## Highlights

- i picture each of us with our own agents. our own explanations. our own little worlds. making more things, with fewer reasons to leave the screen or work through a difference with another person.
- i don’t want a life without struggle. i want people to struggle with.
