## Summary

The brain makes confident guesses before getting full proof, trusting the body’s sensors to check accuracy. AI today acts like a guessing brain but relies on humans to do the checking, reversing their natural roles. The real question is who leads and who verifies, and we should stay in charge of the guessing, not just watch AI guess for us.

## Highlights

- The executive’s chair is where the self does its living: the guessing, the conviction, the freedom to be wrong, to revise, to become someone you weren’t yesterday. Cells don’t get to wonder.
