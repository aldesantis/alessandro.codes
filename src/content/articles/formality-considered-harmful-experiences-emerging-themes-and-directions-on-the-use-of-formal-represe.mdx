## Summary

This paper discusses the challenges users face with formal representations in computer systems and suggests ways for designers to address these issues. Users often struggle with making implicit aspects of their tasks explicit through formalisms, and designers should match the level of formal expression with user goals and situations. The study highlights the importance of incremental formalization strategies to reduce cognitive overhead and improve user interaction with computer systems.

## Highlights

- In other cases, more formalization brings more computational power to bear on the task; idea processors and hypermedia writing tools demand more specification of structure, but they also provide functionality that allows users to reorganize text or present it on-line as a non-linear work.
