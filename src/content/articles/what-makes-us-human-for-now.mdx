## Summary

AI systems today can imitate human language by borrowing meaning from large amounts of human-created content. However, they do not truly understand or create new meaning like humans do. Being human means making and upgrading meaning, which machines cannot yet do.

## Highlights

- What makes us human is our ability to do things which are not-yet-understood, which require us to be able to create meaning where there wasn’t meaning before.
- Machines may be good at figuring out how to achieve a given outcome, but so far only humans can decide that a particular outcome means something and is therefore worth doing.
- **What makes us human changes over time, in relation to what we choose to set ourselves apart from.** Our ability to individually or collectively upgrade what it means to be human as time goes on and the context changes is what makes us human. Again, this is the same thing as the ability to make and give new meaning instead of simply borrowing and reusing old meaning.
