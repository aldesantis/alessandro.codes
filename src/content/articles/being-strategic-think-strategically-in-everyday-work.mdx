## Summary

Being strategic means actively looking for ways to improve and influence your work beyond daily tasks. It involves understanding the bigger picture, managing trade-offs, and communicating thoughtfully across teams. Anyone can learn to think strategically by practicing these skills in everyday situations.

## Highlights

- Situations are not ‘strategic’ or ‘not strategic’ by default. Moments and decisions can _become strategic_ when you use them to change or reinforce direction. Every situation can be examined for signals and opportunities to influence future capabilities, manage organizational risks, improve systemic decisions, raise standards, or adjust other tradeoffs.
