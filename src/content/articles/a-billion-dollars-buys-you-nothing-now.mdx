## Summary

AI tools like coding agents can empower individuals to create more and be more creative. The cost to develop advanced AI models is rising so fast that even well-funded startups struggle to keep up. Robotics and creator platforms are rapidly evolving, reshaping industries and competition.

## Highlights

- In the creator market, and in SMB products generally, the most valuable thing you can offer any merchant under $50 million in revenue is demand generation, and they will accept a staggering amount of fees and humiliation to get it. Ask anyone selling on Amazon or an independent restaurant eating DoorDash fees.
