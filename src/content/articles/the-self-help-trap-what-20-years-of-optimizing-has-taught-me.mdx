## Summary

Self-help can trap people in endless self-improvement without real happiness. True growth comes from focusing on relationships and self-transcendence, not just fixing yourself. Acceptance and connection with others bring peace, not constant self-optimization.

## Highlights

- **Self-transcendence means going beyond the self**—seeking connection with something greater, such as service to others, nature, art, or the divine. Why is it important? Well, for one thing, as [Tony Robbins](https://tim.blog/2014/10/15/money-master-the-game/) put it at an event long ago: “‘I, I, I, me, me, me’ gets to be a really fucking boring song.”
