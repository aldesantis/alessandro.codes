## Summary

Algorithms change often, but their goal is to show people useful, trustworthy content. Focus on your customers and give them content that genuinely helps, rather than chasing every new trick. Stay aware of how content is shared, but never sacrifice the reader’s experience.

## Highlights

- Indeed, it would take a human being impossibly long to parse through all of the information on the internet and find the answer that helps them the most. So, algorithms are a very useful stand-in for human judgment about quality and trust. Their long-term goal, whether it’s an SEO algorithm or an AEO algorithm or the LinkedIn algorithm or the next algorithm that nobody has invented yet, is to surface content people find worthwhile.
- No channels truly ever die. I still get glossy magazines in the snail mail for DTC products all the time. But channels can get so noisy that we _need_ the algorithms. They are filters to keep the channel usable. If we want to participate in the channel, we have to respect the algorithm.
