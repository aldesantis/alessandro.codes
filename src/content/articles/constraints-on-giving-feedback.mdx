## Summary

Giving feedback is important, but it can damage relationships if not done carefully. It’s better to focus on helping your team succeed in their current environment rather than just pointing out problems. Effective feedback should be clear and well-structured, while also maintaining strong relationships for better communication.

## Highlights

- You may have to acknowledge the problems around you to maintain credibility, but done frequently that creates a [values oasis](https://lethain.com/values-oasis/). It also frames you as an observer of the problem rather than part of the solution, which diminishes you in the eyes of whoever you’re talking with.
