## Summary

Meta has launched Muse, an AI agent that can shop, message, and plan activities for users on WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook. Muse can make purchases with user approval and uses social data to personalize recommendations. However, trust and privacy concerns remain a challenge for widespread adoption.

## Highlights

- “Every great shift in commerce has been, at heart, a shift in trust. From the marketplace to the mall, to the catalog, to the click of a button, each historic evolution of commerce has asked people to place trust somewhere new.”
