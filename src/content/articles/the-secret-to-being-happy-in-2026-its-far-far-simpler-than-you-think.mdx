## Summary

Spend 2026 doing more of what truly makes you come alive instead of endlessly trying to fix yourself. Engaging activities naturally crowd out bad habits and reduce overwhelm. Life’s purpose is to feel alive now, not to wait until you’re “ready.”

## Highlights

- “If we want our children to revel in reality, then the most effective way to teach them is to do it ourselves … My advice is to fight [internet dependency] less with fleeting (and unreliable) hacks like time limits, tech‑free zones, digital detoxes, tech fasts, focus mode, and grayscaled screens, and more with an overwhelming love and appreciation for being present, active, and engaged with the real world.”
