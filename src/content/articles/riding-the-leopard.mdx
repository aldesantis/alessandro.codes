## Summary

The meaning of life is to experience and create the world in a unique way. We are the universe looking at itself through many eyes, each with a special role. This gives us a duty to be our fullest, most original selves to help the universe grow.

## Highlights

- You are god, the universe, whatever, looking at itself through a particular set of eyes, experiencing itself through a particular consciousness.
