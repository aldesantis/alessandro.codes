## Summary

AI helps developers work faster, but many companies don’t see real benefits because their processes aren’t ready. Successful AI use needs strong testing, clear context, and good team practices to avoid mistakes and delays. Teams that focus on improving their workflows and tools together get the most value from AI.

## Highlights

- The [2025 DORA report introduced seven distinct team archetypes](https://www.faros.ai/blog/key-takeaways-from-the-dora-report-2025) that reveal how organizational health, team structure, and platform maturity influence performance outcomes:
  1. **Foundational Challenges** - Teams in survival mode with significant process gaps
  2. **Legacy Bottleneck** - Constantly reacting to unstable systems where AI makes individuals faster but outdated deployment systems and messy integrations eat up all those gains
  3. **Constrained by Process** - Consumed by inefficient workflows
  4. **High Impact, Low Cadence** - Producing quality work slowly
  5. **Stable and Methodical** - Delivering deliberately with high quality
  6. **Pragmatic Performers** - Impressive speed with functional environments
  7. **Harmonious High-Achievers** - A virtuous cycle of sustainable excellence
- The speed-versus-stability trade-off is a myth. The best-performing teams achieve both high throughput and high stability simultaneously, while struggling teams often fail at both dimensions.
