## Summary

Google’s Universal Cart gives shoppers a shared checkout, but may pull them away from merchants’ own stores.
Shopify is taking the opposite approach by letting many AI agents browse products and pay with Shop Pay.
The newsletter explores how AI is reshaping online shopping, while platforms compete for control and sales.

## Highlights

- Google used to give directions. Now it’s built a dome around the city and has declared itself the destination. And Shopify doesn’t care, as long as it keeps getting a slice of the payments. What happened to arming the rebels?
- Not only is Shopify welcoming agents, it’s also laying the groundwork for them to make money, like digital influencers. Through a program called Promoted Placements, currently in an invite-only preview, developers can show promoted products in their agent’s search results and earn a commission when shoppers buy them. Shopify is paying a 0.3% base commission for now, which comes out of its payment processing fee (ie: not paid by the merchant), but merchants will eventually be able to add their own commission on top.
