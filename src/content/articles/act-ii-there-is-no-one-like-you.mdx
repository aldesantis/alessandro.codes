## Summary

Sublime is a creative platform built to help people see their unique selves by cutting through online noise. Its story is deeply tied to love and the personal journey of its founders. The message is clear: there is no one like you, and creating is how you reveal that truth.

## Highlights

- We know that the journey is the destination, yet we keep buying into the idea that we can bypass the process.
- Our nature—mind, nervous system, body, and spirit—are designed for and fulfilled by the step-by-step. By experiencing, feeling, embodying, and digesting how we get from one thing to another. Yet we fall into the trap of believing there is a shortcut for the well done.
