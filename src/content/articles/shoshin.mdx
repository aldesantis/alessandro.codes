## Summary

Shoshin means “beginner’s mind,” a concept from Zen Buddhism. It encourages staying open and curious, like a beginner. This idea is discussed in the works of Master Dogen and other authors.

## Highlights

- In Japanese calligraphy _shoshin_ is not an aesthetic choice, but rather a state of mind of the practitioner, one that addresses and counters the paradox within calligraphy where the more one practices, the more the quality of the calligraphy may suffer.
