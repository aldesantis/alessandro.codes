## Summary

Experts often make decisions by recognizing familiar patterns, not by weighing options. Training should build experience and teach people to read the cues that matter in their field. Finding the right way to frame a problem often makes its solution clear.

## Highlights

- It took me a while to notice the contrast between a client’s initial confusion when they approached us saying they didn’t even understand the nature of the problem, and when we finished the project and they told us the solutions were so sensible that they didn’t need testing. It was only once I started really appreciating that change that I came to realize that the value we were providing wasn’t the solutions, but a mental model of the problem that was so clear and precise that the solutions tended to reveal themselves.
