## Summary

Platter is a New York agency that builds and improves Shopify stores, promising better profits at lower cost. Its growth depends heavily on CEO Ben Sharf’s personal content and founder network. Platter also uses events and partner campaigns to attract clients, while Nebulab could learn from its case studies and targeted gatherings.

## Highlights

- Events are a frequent, cheap NYC community machine: at least 15 Luma events in 18 months. Most are sauna mornings, poker nights and rooftop drinks for 10–30 founders, some co-hosted with DTC apps (Skio, Rivo, Superfiliate). The biggest was a panel at Shopify NY with 194 attendees. No sponsorships or conference talks.[37]
