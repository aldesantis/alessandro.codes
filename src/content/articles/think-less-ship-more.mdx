## Summary

Jeff encourages agency owners to stop overthinking and start taking action quickly. He shares how shipping work regularly leads to success and growth. The message is: do more, worry less, and learn as you go.

## Highlights

- Agencies are no different. The new service line. The hire you keep not making. The podcast. God, the podcast. I can’t count how many people have told me “I’ve been thinking about starting a podcast.” Oh yeah? How long? “Couple years.” That person loses every time to the one who just says screw it and hits record. And nine times out of ten the scrappy thing they actually ship beats the perfect version they were never going to finish anyway.
