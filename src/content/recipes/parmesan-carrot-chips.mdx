## Steps

1. Peel the carrots, halve them crosswise, then cut each half lengthwise into quarters, so each carrot yields eight sticks.
2. In a bowl, mix the oil, paprika, pepper, garlic, chili flakes, salt, and a splash of water.
3. Toss the carrot sticks in the seasoning, rubbing it in by hand until evenly coated.
4. Dip each stick in a small bowl of grated parmesan.
5. Arrange the sticks on a parchment-lined baking tray, spaced apart.
6. Spray with a light mist of olive oil and bake in a fan oven at 200 °C for 40 minutes.
