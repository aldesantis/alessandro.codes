A lightened-up burrito with a Crispy McBacon flavor profile: extra-lean beef, a tangy gherkin sauce, protein cheese, and air-fried crispy bacon.

## Steps

1. Soften the diced onion in a pan with a splash of water.
2. Once softened, add the tomato paste and toast it, then add the ground beef and cook over high heat with the salt and pepper.
3. For the sauce, chop the gherkins and mix them with all the other sauce ingredients in a bowl.
4. Off the heat, stir the sauce into the meat and mix well.
5. Crisp the bacon in an air fryer at 200°C for about 6 minutes, then chop it finely.
6. Fill the tortillas with the meat in the center, the grated cheese, and the bacon. To make them easier to handle, warm them for 15 seconds in the microwave first.
7. Once closed, sear the burritos in a hot pan over high heat to seal them.

## Notes

- One burrito of six: about 320 kcal — 35 g carbs, 25 g protein, 10.2 g fat.
- The source uses an olive oil cooking spray for the light cooking steps.
