## Steps

1. Make the [[brava-sauce|brava sauce]].
2. Preheat the oven to 220 °C (fan) and line a baking tray with parchment paper.
3. Peel the potatoes and cut them into cubes.
4. Toss the potatoes with a generous drizzle of olive oil and salt, then spread them in a single layer on the tray.
5. Bake for 35-40 minutes, turning halfway through, until golden and crisp.
6. Serve the patatas bravas in a bowl, generously topped with brava sauce, and finish with fresh chives.
