## Steps

1. Shave the Parmesan into flakes and set aside.
2. Cook the chicken steak in a pan.
3. Toast the two slices of bread.
4. Spread a tablespoon of mayonnaise on each slice, then layer on the lamb’s lettuce, the chicken steak, and the Parmesan flakes. Close the sandwich and serve.
