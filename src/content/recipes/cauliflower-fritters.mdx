## Steps

1. Mash the steamed cauliflower florets with a fork in a bowl.
2. Add the grated Parmigiano and the salt.
3. Add the pepper, eggs, and flour, and mix until smooth.
4. Heat a little oil in a nonstick pan. Drop three to four tablespoons of the mixture per fritter and cook over medium heat for about two minutes per side, until golden.
5. Serve hot, optionally with a yogurt sauce.

## Notes

- Best eaten freshly made. Leftovers keep in the fridge, in an airtight container, for one to two days.
- To bake instead of fry, arrange the fritters on a baking sheet lined with parchment, drizzle with oil, and bake at 200°C for 15–20 minutes, turning halfway.
- Spices or herbs such as paprika, nutmeg, turmeric, parsley, or thyme can be added to the mixture.
