## Steps

1. Mash the steamed cauliflower florets with a fork in a bowl.
2. Add the grated Parmigiano and the salt.
3. Add the pepper, eggs, and flour, and mix until smooth.
4. Preheat the oven to 200°C and line a baking sheet with parchment.
5. Set an 8 cm round pastry ring on the sheet and fill it with the mixture to a thickness of 0.5 cm, leveling it with the back of a spoon. Lift the ring and repeat, spacing the fritters slightly apart. The mixture makes about twenty-four fritters.
6. Brush the tops with oil and bake for 12–15 minutes, turning halfway, until golden on both sides.
7. Serve hot, optionally with a yogurt sauce or [[aioli-sauce|garlic mayonnaise]].

## Notes

- Best eaten freshly made. Leftovers keep in the fridge, in an airtight container, for one to two days.
- To fry instead of bake, heat a little oil in a nonstick pan, shape the fritters the same way, and cook over medium heat for about two minutes per side, until golden.
- Spices or herbs such as paprika, nutmeg, turmeric, parsley, or thyme can be added to the mixture.
