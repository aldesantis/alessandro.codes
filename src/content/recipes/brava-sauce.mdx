## Steps

1. Finely chop the onion.
2. Finely chop the peeled garlic.
3. In a pan with a generous amount of olive oil, gently cook the onion and garlic with half the water for 5 minutes.
4. Add the apple cider vinegar, smoked paprika, and flour.
5. Mix well, add the remaining water, salt to taste, and cook for 6-7 minutes, or until thick and creamy.
6. Transfer the sauce to an immersion blender cup and blend until smooth.
