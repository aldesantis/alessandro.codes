## Steps

1. Brew 70 g of coffee with a moka pot, stir in the sugar until no sediment remains at the bottom, then let it cool to room temperature.
2. Chill the sweetened coffee in the fridge for at least 2 hours.
3. Pour the cold cream, straight from the fridge, into a bowl and begin whipping it with an electric mixer.
4. As soon as it starts to thicken slightly, pour in the cold coffee in a thin stream while keeping the beaters running.
5. Keep working the mixture until it reaches a foamy consistency, being careful not to over-whip; finish by hand with a whisk to avoid this. Serve immediately, or cover and refrigerate.

## Notes

- Do not reduce the sugar: it is not just for sweetness but is essential to the cream’s structure.
- Dust with ground cinnamon or unsweetened cocoa powder before serving, if you like.
- Keeps for one day in the fridge; stir gently with a whisk before serving to revive it. Freezing is not recommended.
