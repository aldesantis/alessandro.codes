## Steps

1. Preheat the sous vide water bath to 58 °C.
2. Remove any cartilage or fatty sinew from the veal roast, then season it all over with salt.
3. Place the meat in a vacuum bag with the garlic, bay leaf, cloves, a few black peppercorns, and the olive oil, and seal.
4. Cook in the water bath for 4 hours (at least 3 for a roast about 7 cm thick).
5. Open the bag, strain the cooking juices, and set them aside for the sauce, topping up with broth to reach the 150 g it needs.
6. Pat the meat dry and sear it briefly on all sides in a very hot, dry pan, about 1 minute in total. The film of oil left from the bag is enough.
7. Let the meat cool, then wrap it tightly and refrigerate until completely cold (ideally overnight).
8. While the meat chills, make the tuna sauce with the reserved cooking juices (see [[tonnato-sauce|Tonnato sauce]]).
9. Once the meat is completely cold, slice it thinly with a smooth-bladed knife.
10. Arrange the slices on a serving platter, spoon the tuna sauce generously over the centre, and decorate with whole and halved caper berries.

## Notes

- For a pinker result, cook at 56 °C for 6–7 hours; for even more tender meat, cook at 54 °C for up to 20 hours.
- The wine and the carrot, celery and onion from the poaching method are left out because alcohol doesn’t cook off in a sealed bag and raw aromatics come out harsh. The sear is optional, but it adds flavour and a neater edge to the slices.
