# Tonnato veal

## Steps

1. Make the tuna sauce — see [[tonnato-sauce|Tonnato sauce]].
2. Preheat the sous vide water bath to 58 °C.
3. Remove any cartilage or fatty sinew from the veal roast, then season it all over with salt.
4. Place the meat in a vacuum bag with the garlic, bay leaf, cloves, a few black peppercorns, and the olive oil, and seal.
5. Cook in the water bath for 5 hours.
6. Open the bag, strain the cooking juices, and set them aside for the sauce, topping up with broth to reach the 150 g it needs.
7. Pat the meat dry and sear it briefly on all sides in a very hot pan, about 1 minute in total.
8. Let the meat cool, then wrap it tightly and refrigerate until completely cold (ideally overnight).
9. Once the meat is completely cold, slice it thinly with a smooth-bladed knife.
10. Arrange the slices on a serving platter, spoon the tuna sauce generously over the centre, and decorate with whole and halved caper berries.

## Notes

- For a pinker result, cook at 56 °C for 6–7 hours; for even more tender meat, cook at 54 °C for up to 20 hours.
- The wine and the carrot, celery and onion from the poaching method are left out because alcohol doesn’t cook off in a sealed bag and raw aromatics come out harsh. The sear is optional, but it adds flavour and a neater edge to the slices.
