A spiced, Indian-inspired vegan dahl from [GialloZafferano](https://ricette.giallozafferano.it/Dahl-di-lenticchie-rosse.html), served with basmati rice.

## Steps

1. Boil the lentils in plenty of water for the time indicated on the package.
2. Meanwhile, finely chop the onion and the ginger.
3. Heat a drizzle of oil in a saucepan, add the onion and ginger, and let them soften gently.
4. Toast the cumin seeds in a separate pan.
5. Drain the lentils and add them to the saucepan.
6. Stir in the tomato passata, the water, and the coconut milk.
7. Add the toasted cumin seeds, the turmeric, the garlic powder, the garam masala, and the chili powder.
8. Stir well and season with salt to taste.
9. Simmer over low heat for about an hour and a half, until the lentils are creamy.
10. Meanwhile, bring a pot of salted water to a boil, cook the basmati rice for the time indicated on the package, and drain it.
11. Serve the dahl with the basmati rice.
