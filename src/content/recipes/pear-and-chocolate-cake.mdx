A creamy, moist cake from [Fatto in casa da Benedetta](https://www.fattoincasadabenedetta.it/ricetta/torta-cremosa-cioccolato-e-pere/).

## Steps

1. Roughly chop the dark chocolate and cut the pears into small pieces.
2. Crack the eggs into a bowl, add the sugar and sunflower oil, and stir to combine.
3. Add the flour and baking powder and stir again.
4. Pour in the milk and stir until the batter is smooth.
5. Fold in the pear pieces and the chopped chocolate, mixing well.
6. Line the base of a 22 cm springform pan with baking paper, pour in the batter, and sprinkle the chocolate chips over the surface.
7. Bake in a fan oven preheated to 180°C for 45–50 minutes, or in a static oven at 190°C for the same time.
