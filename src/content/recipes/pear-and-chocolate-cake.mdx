## Steps

1. **Caramelize the pears.** Melt the butter with the brown sugar in a wide pan. Add the pears and cook for 6–8 minutes, turning, until golden and just tender. Add the lemon juice and let cool. Keep the syrup.
2. **Brown the butter.** Cook it over medium heat until it smells nutty and turns deep golden, 5–7 minutes. Take it off the heat, add the chopped chocolate, and stir until smooth.
3. **Whip the eggs.** Beat the eggs with both sugars for about 5 minutes, until pale and thick. Mix in the vanilla, salt, and chocolate butter, then the yogurt.
4. **Add the dry ingredients.** Sift in the flour, almond flour, cocoa, and baking powder and fold until just combined. Fold in half the chocolate chunks and any chopped pear trimmings.
5. **Assemble.** Pour the batter into a lined 22 cm springform pan. Fan the pear wedges on top without pressing them in. Scatter over the remaining chocolate and drizzle with the pear syrup.
6. **Bake** in a static oven at 170°C for 45–55 minutes, until a toothpick comes out with a few moist crumbs.
7. **Cool** for at least 30 minutes before unmolding. The flavor deepens by the next day.
