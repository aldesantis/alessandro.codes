Pasta al pomodoro crudo: an uncooked datterino tomato sauce blitzed with basil, olive oil, and Parmigiano, finished with stracciatella.

## Steps

1. Blend the raw datterini with the olive oil, basil leaves, Parmigiano, salt, and a pinch of sugar into a smooth, creamy sauce.
2. Cook the pasta in salted water, drain, and toss it off the heat with the raw tomato sauce.
3. Plate with stracciatella, a drizzle of raw olive oil, and plenty of basil.
