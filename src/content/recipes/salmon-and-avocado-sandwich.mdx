Smoked salmon, avocado, salad greens, and cherry tomatoes

## Steps

1. Toast the bread slices until golden and crisp.
2. Halve the avocado, remove the pit, and scoop the flesh into a bowl. Mash it with a fork together with the lemon juice, a pinch of salt, and a little pepper.
3. Halve the cherry tomatoes and season them with a drizzle of olive oil and a pinch of salt.
4. Spread a generous layer of the avocado on two slices of toast. Top with the salad greens, then the smoked salmon, then the cherry tomatoes.
5. Finish with a little more pepper, close with the remaining slices of toast, and cut each sandwich in half to serve.
