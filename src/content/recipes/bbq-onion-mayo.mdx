## Steps

1. Slice the onion fairly thin, but not too thin. Put the onion in a pan with the oil and salt and brown over medium-high heat for a few minutes, stirring constantly.
2. Once darkened, add the water and honey and keep cooking over medium heat until caramelized.
3. Toward the end, stir in the BBQ sauce; once combined, turn off the heat.
4. Transfer the onion and all the base ingredients to a blender and blend until smooth.
