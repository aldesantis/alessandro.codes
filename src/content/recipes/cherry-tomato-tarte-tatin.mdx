An upside-down savory tart: cherry tomatoes caramelized under a mustard-brushed sheet of puff pastry.

## Steps

1. Wash the cherry tomatoes, pat them dry, and cut them in half. Toss them in a bowl with the sliced garlic, olive oil, salt, freshly ground pepper, and the basil torn by hand.
2. Line a 24 cm round pan with parchment paper and scatter it with flakes of butter and the brown sugar.
3. Drain the tomatoes well of their juices and arrange them in the pan, cut side up.
4. Unroll the puff pastry and spread it with the mustard. Lay it over the tomatoes, tucking the edges down well inside the pan, and prick it all over with a fork.
5. Bake in a preheated oven at 200°C for about 25–30 minutes, until the pastry is golden.
6. Let the tart rest for about 5 minutes, then place a plate over the pan and flip it, watching out for hot juices. Remove the parchment, nudge any tomatoes back into place, and serve.

## Notes

- Works as an aperitivo with friends or as an unusual side dish.
