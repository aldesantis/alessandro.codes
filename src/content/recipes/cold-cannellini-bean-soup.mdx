A no-cook summer soup: canned cannellini beans blended with raw tomato, red onion, and basil, served cold. Ready in about three minutes.

## Steps

1. Rinse the cannellini beans well.
2. Blend the beans with the tomatoes, red onion, basil, garlic (if using), water, salt, and pepper until smooth and creamy.
3. Serve cold, with toasted bread cubed into the soup or on the side for dipping.

## Notes

- Everything goes in raw — the only cooked ingredient is the canned beans.
- Also works with chickpeas or lentils in place of the cannellini.
