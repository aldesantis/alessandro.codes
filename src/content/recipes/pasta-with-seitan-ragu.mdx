## Steps

1. Roughly chop the celery, carrot, onion, and seitan, then pulse them for a few seconds in a mixer.
2. Take a large pan, add plenty of oil, the chopped rosemary, and the vegetable and seitan mix. Season with salt and cook for a few minutes.
3. Add the tomato passata and the hot water and continue cooking, stirring occasionally. Towards the end, add 15 ml of soy sauce.
4. Cook the pasta in a pot with plenty of salted water for the time indicated on the package.
5. Once ready, drain it and pour it directly into the pan with the seitan ragù, mixing well.
6. Serve the pasta and finish with a little vegan grated cheese.
