If the goal of your e-commerce replatform is to simply copy-paste what you have over to the new platform, you’re getting it wrong.

Instead, you should treat the replatform as a catalyst: use this moment to take stock of your entire digital operation and understand what truly adds value and what is historical cruft you can get rid of.

In turn, this will allow you to drastically simplify your replatforming project.
