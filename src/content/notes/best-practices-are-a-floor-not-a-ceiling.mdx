Best practices are a floor, not a ceiling. You should ensure you understand them and their rationale (because [[learn-the-rules-before-breaking-them|Understand the rules before breaking them]]), but you should also feel free to carefully and deliberately break them if you think they’re not a good fit for your context.

This applies to the functions you compete on. For the ones you don’t, the floor _is_ the target: there’s no reason to differ from what everyone else does, which is why [[necessities-converge-on-the-standard-answer|necessities converge on the standard answer]].
