The only thing that makes freedom valuable is that we can give it up for something we believe in. Freedom is essentially useless if you don’t use it for something you care about, but instead keep all your options open.

This is true in all aspects of life. A few examples:

- [[structure-sets-you-free|Structure sets you free]]. While some people hate bureaucracy, the _right_ amount of process and bureaucracy unlocks your time and mental space to focus on the few things that truly matter.
- [[optionality-can-hinder-risk-taking|Optionality can hinder risk-taking]]. When you keep all your options open endlessly, you spread yourself too thin and rob yourself of the opportunity to commit to something.

At the same time, you should always remember that [[compliance-is-not-commitment|compliance is not commitment]]: it is one thing to willingly give up your freedom in pursuit of a vision; it is another to have your freedom unwillingly taken from you.
