Sometimes, your emotions may lead you astray.

Emotions are triggers/warning signs, and they should be used like data: just like you should be data-informed, not data-driven, you should also be emotion-informed, not emotion-driven.

An example is [[defensiveness-inhibits-learning|getting defensive]] because you feel that your authority is being threatened: by closing yourself off to the other person rather than learning from them, you are missing an important growth opportunity.
