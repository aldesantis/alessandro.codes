We all have weird thoughts going through our minds, i.e., things that are not aligned with conventional or acceptable wisdom.

When we suppress them and fall in line, we lose our distinctiveness and, therefore, our power.

Instead, we need to listen to the weird thoughts and understand how we can use them to maximize our impact on the world.

Weird thoughts are worth following because [[Interestingness comes from within|interestingness comes from within]]: an idea is interesting because of how _we_ respond to it, not because of the idea itself. They are also what makes us funny, because [[weirdness-is-the-seed-of-humor|weirdness is the seed of humor]].

The same applies to companies. Conventional wisdom is available to every competitor, but [[strategy-requires-original-insights|strategy requires original insights]], which is why [[all-good-strategies-are-unique|all good strategies are unique]].
