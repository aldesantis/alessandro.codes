People gossip because they feel a lack of control over their situation. Gossiping is a way to feel included and validated, to feel right and make others wrong, and to gain more control over the outcomes of the situation.

In other words, people gossip out of fear when they lack [[psychological-safety-is-a-team-factor|psychological safety.]]
