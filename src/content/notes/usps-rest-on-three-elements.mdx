A company’s unique selling proposition (USP) rests on three elements:

- **Resonance:** Does the USP resonate with the customer? Is it something that they will care about, or is it just fluffy marketing speak?
- **Differentiation:** Is the USP meaningfully different from your competitors? Or is it the same as everyone else’s?
- **Ability to substantiate:** Can you produce proof of the USP? More importantly, can you produce proof _before_ the sale or only after the sale?
