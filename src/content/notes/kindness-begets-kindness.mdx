If you ask someone for a small favor and they agree, it’s more likely that the person will then agree to a slightly larger favor, and so on and so forth. It’s a [[reinforcing-feedback-loops-can-be-self-enhancing-or-self-destructive|virtuous reinforcing feedback loop]].
