Getting what you want is a sign of intelligence.

Knowing what’s worth wanting is a sign of wisdom.

This is a useful general life principle, but it also applies to certain cultural fragments. For example, see how [[the-new-luxury-is-about-taste-not-wealth|the new luxury is about taste, not wealth]].

In business, knowing what’s worth wanting is what Vaughn Tan calls _meaningmaking_, and it’s the part of work AI can’t do yet: [[execution-is-translation-strategy-is-meaningmaking|execution is translation, strategy is meaningmaking]].
