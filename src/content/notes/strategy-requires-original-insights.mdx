Strategy requires someone with a deep understanding of the subject matter to have an original insight.

The insight has to be original because an advantage has to be [[efficiency-must-be-defensible|defensible]]: anything everyone else can arrive at is an advantage to no one. This is why the insight can’t come from a model that every competitor also has access to.

And it has to come from deep understanding because insight grows out of context, and the most valuable context is unique, mostly [[tacit-knowledge-is-hard-to-capture|tacit]], and [[context-is-relational|relational]]: it comes from the meeting between a company and the people in it.

This holds even for advantages that don’t look like insight. Network effects, distribution, proprietary data, or a regulatory moat can all be sources of advantage, but someone first has to see that they matter here and how to build them—and that is the original insight.
